About Rufin

Rufin is a fast, native music player for local folders, network shares and music servers, on Linux, Windows, macOS and Android. It offers deep customization for its appearance and behavior, a rich library browsing and management experience with in-app lyrics, metadata and artwork searching & editing, and a careful playback engine powered by GStreamer.

Rufin Connect securely syncs your devices and moves playback between them, and Rooms let you listen together with friends; Rufin can also run headless, be scripted, or be controlled from its Web UI.

Downloaded tracks, and tracks of a server matched to local folders, play from disk while activity still goes to the server. You can also configure a local and public address for a server.

Features

Playback

  • Recommendations: configurable recommendation engine powered by ListenBrainz for queue and track radios
  • Queue: Auto DJ keeps it filled with recommendations, skips unavailable tracks automatically, sleep timer
  • Transitions: true gapless playback or equal-power crossfade, with an optional fade on play/pause
  • Loudness: ReplayGain and EBU R128 normalization with true-peak analysis; results can be written to tags
  • Sound: graphic (10, 18 or 31 bands) and parametric equalizer with saved presets per device and AutoEQ/APO import, crossfeed, playback speed and pitch
  • Radio: select a configured radio station or add one yourself; artwork and lyrics are resolved best-effort, with synced lyrics for LISTEN.moe
  • Output: audio device selection, multichannel output and Dolby/DTS bitstream passthrough, casting to UPnP and Chromecast
  • Formats: broad codec support and CUE sheets as separate playable tracks
    Supported formats

    AAC and ALAC (including M4A/MP4), AIFF and WAV, APE, FLAC, TTA and WavPack, MP1/MP2/MP3 and Musepack, Ogg Vorbis/Opus/Speex, MKA and audio-only WebM, WMA/ASF, DSD, tracker modules such as MOD/XM/IT/S3M/MPTM, and emulated game music such as NSF/VGM.

Rufin Connect

  • Syncs sources, credentials, playlists, favorites, pins, preferences and listening history between your devices; queues, downloads and local paths stay per device
  • Continue playback where another device left off, or control what another device is playing
  • Stream your local music library to another device
  • Devices pair with emoji verification (like Matrix); connections between them are end-to-end encrypted (Iroh) and go directly where possible, and a relay you choose helps devices find each other; an encrypted sync folder (local, WebDAV or SMB) covers devices that are never online together

Rooms

  • Listen together: create a room and share its invite link; everyone hears the same queue in step, each on their own device
  • Everyone can play, pause, seek, skip, add and reorder songs; the host ends the room, and leaving gives you your own queue back
  • Each listener plays their own copy of a song, from whichever source they have it in; with "Share songs" on, members who have a song play it for listeners who don't
  • No account: every room has a new identity, its invite stops working when the room ends, and a listener whose connection drops is brought back automatically
  • Connections between devices are end-to-end encrypted (Iroh) and go directly where possible; a relay the host chooses helps devices find each other, and music never passes through a public relay

Integrations

  • Scrobbling: Last.fm, Libre.fm, ListenBrainz, and self-hosted servers with offline storage and automatic retries; Discord Rich Presence
  • System: media controls (MPRIS with lyrics on Linux), tray icon, running in the background; credentials are kept in the system keyring
  • Private mode: temporarily pauses external activity
  • Controller: control playback, browse the library, manage sources and playlists from a browser or the HTTP API, with token authentication; playback stays on the device running Rufin. It also runs without the desktop interface: 
    export RUFIN_API_TOKEN="your_token"
rufin --headless --listen

Library

  • Browsing: a customizable Home, Folders, Genres, Moods, History and pins; details pages for tracks, albums and artists
  • Search: search within a route or globally, optionally matching songs by their lyrics
  • Playlists: mix tracks from different sources, organize playlists in folders, import and export M3U, PLS and XSPF
  • Smart playlists: rules on metadata, mood/BPM and play/skip history, with custom sorting and limits
  • Recommendations: track, album, artist, playlist and genre radios from the server or a local fallback
  • Downloads: manual or through automatic rules, with optional transcoding; downloaded music stays playable offline
  • Metadata: tag and artwork editor with batch editing, MusicBrainz and AcoustID lookup with on-device fingerprinting; missing artwork and lyrics are fetched automatically
  • Lyrics: synced and word-by-word lyrics with karaoke mode, Furigana/Romaji, translations, online search, editable text and offset; saved as embedded tags or .lrc files
  • Activity: favorites, ratings and listening history; a listening overview you can export as an image, and history as Last.fm/ListenBrainz compatible .csv
  • Sharing: share server links of your tracks, or share the song or selected lyrics as an elegant card for social media on Android
  • Backups: granular import/export including credentials; scheduled and password-protected backups
  • Files: open audio files and playlists as the default player, from the CLI with rufin track.mp4, or by dragging them into the window

Interface

  • Desktop: adaptive layout with an extra mini player, fullscreen player with visualizer and lyrics, themes including custom ones, and an extensive keyboard shortcuts catalog
  • Android: built with Jetpack Compose and reliable in layouts beyond the regular vertical screen: landscape mode, foldables, tablets and TVs
