# Downloads

In the `Preferences -> Downloads` tab, downloads can be enabled, to listen to music without internet.

## Manual Downloads

For manual downloads, just rightclick the song you wish to download and it should be added to your folder automatically.

## Automatic Downloads

Automatic downloads can be achived by selecting a proper download rule from the list of possible ones.

- Entire library option will download your entire library to your device
- Favourites option will download only favourited tracks.
- All playlists option will download all songs from your playlists.
- 5 latest albums option will download 5 albums, that were recently played.

## Downloads Format
For people with slower internet or not much space on prefered device, it is recomended to change this setting into something that will compress the audio beforehand. This will result in saved space with not much quality lost.
